Overview

The Role

As Campus-U Marketing Coordinator, I serve as the on-the-ground representative for NBCUniversal's college marketing program at MSU — connecting national entertainment brands with a highly engaged student audience through authentic, culturally relevant activations.

The Challenge

College audiences are notoriously hard to reach through traditional advertising. The challenge was creating genuine moments of discovery that felt native to campus life rather than corporate — turning brand exposure into real enthusiasm.

Signature Activation

B1G Preview × The Paper — Campus Screening

A first-of-its-kind branded campus event

Collaborated with Peacock and NBC Sports to execute a campus screening of Universal Pictures' The Paper with live branded integration tied to NBC Sports' annual B1G Preview — running concurrent with an MSU football game. The event merged live sports culture with a theatrical premiere experience, creating a dual-screen moment unique to the MSU campus.

Ongoing Responsibilities

Social Campaigns

Manage multi-platform social campaigns for NBC Sports programming, Universal Pictures theatrical releases, and Peacock original content — tailoring each campaign to resonate with an 18–24 demographic.

Partner Coordination

Build and maintain relationships with student organizations, campus media outlets, and local retailers to amplify promotional reach beyond direct-to-consumer channels.

Live Activations

Conceive and execute themed events, screenings, and guerrilla marketing stunts designed to generate genuine word-of-mouth and social sharing among students.
