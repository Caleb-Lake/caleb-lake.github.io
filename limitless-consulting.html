The Role

Director of Content

As Director of Content, I own the entire creative output of Limitless Consulting — from defining the brand's visual and verbal identity to producing the videos, graphics, and social content that represent the organization externally and internally.

The Challenge

Student organizations often suffer from inconsistent branding — different aesthetics every semester, no defined voice, content that doesn't reflect the quality of the work being done. The goal was to professionalize the brand without losing the approachability that makes a club feel welcoming.

Brand Pillars

🎯

Clarity

Every piece of content communicates a single clear message — no clutter, no ambiguity.

🤝

Approachability

Professional without being corporate — a tone that welcomes students at every level.

✨

Quality

Polished production values that reflect the caliber of consulting work the club produces.

🔄

Consistency

A unified visual system across all platforms so the brand is immediately recognizable.

What I Do

Brand Identity

Defined and maintain the organization's visual language — color systems, typography, graphic templates — ensuring every digital touchpoint looks and feels like Limitless.

Multimedia Content

Produce promotional videos, meeting recap reels, and event coverage using Adobe Premiere and After Effects — creating a growing content library that tells the organization's story over time.

Mentorship

Provide one-on-one coaching to members on marketing fundamentals and consulting strategy — helping newer members develop skills they can apply both in the club and in professional settings.

Leadership & Strategy

Aligning content with organizational goals

Work directly with the executive team to ensure content strategy ladders up to the club's broader objectives — whether that's recruiting new members, promoting client work, or building the organization's reputation on campus. Content isn't produced in a vacuum; every piece serves a clear strategic purpose.

Skills Applied

Creative

Adobe Creative Cloud (Premiere, After Effects, Illustrator, Photoshop), graphic design systems, video production and editing, motion graphics fundamentals.

Strategic

Brand architecture, content calendar planning, audience analysis, cross-platform consistency, stakeholder communication, mentorship and leadership development.
