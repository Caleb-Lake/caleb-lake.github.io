About Me

Passionate about building what's next through creative thinking and modern marketing.

Why do what other people are doing when you can create new ideas and build off the future? I'm Caleb — a marketing student at Michigan State University fascinated by the intersection of technology and brand storytelling.

My path hasn't been linear. Working as an Overnight Camp Counselor at Howell Nature Center taught me patience, adaptability, and how to connect with people from all walks of life. Those same qualities now drive how I approach every marketing challenge.

In high school I co-created CSI Fridays — a weekly broadcast produced for 400+ students. That experience of building an audience from scratch, under a tight deadline, every single week, shaped my instincts as a content creator more than anything else.

At Michigan State I've channeled that energy into real-world roles: running social media for the MSU STARX engineering team, leading content at Limitless Consulting, and interning at both MSU Veterinary Medicine and NBCUniversal.
